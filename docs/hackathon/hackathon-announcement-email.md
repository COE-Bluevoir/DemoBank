## Subject

`Bluevoir AI + Pega Hackathon 2026 | Build Bold. Build Relevant. Build Working Prototypes.`

## Email Draft

Hi Team,

We are excited to launch the **Bluevoir AI + Pega Hackathon 2026**.

This is an internal innovation sprint designed to bring together bold ideas, practical business thinking, and working prototypes that Bluevoir can proudly showcase.

## What is the challenge?

Build a **working prototype** that combines:

- meaningful **AI-driven innovation**
- strong **business relevance**
- and, wherever it adds value, **Pega-enabled or hybrid enterprise architecture**

## Focus areas

- BFSI is the **preferred industry focus**
- Non-BFSI ideas are also allowed if they are strategically relevant to Bluevoir and create strong showcase value
- **AI is mandatory**
- **Pega is strongly encouraged**
- **Hybrid applications are strongly encouraged**

## Team structure

- 10–11 teams
- 3–4 members per team
- teams are already assigned

## Hackathon format

### Kickoff

`Saturday, August 8, 2026`

Teams will align on:

- problem statement
- use case
- architecture direction
- build scope

### Build sprint

`August 9 to August 14, 2026`

### Final prototype demos

`Saturday, August 15, 2026`

## What must each team deliver?

By kickoff day:

- concept submission

By final demo day:

- working prototype
- short architecture summary
- demo flow
- business impact explanation

## What are we looking for?

We want solutions that are:

- innovative
- practical
- relevant to Bluevoir
- strong in AI usage
- strong in business value
- strong in showcase potential

## Rewards

The **top 3 teams** will win prizes.

Further details on judges and prizes will be shared separately.

## One key reminder

This is **not** just an idea contest.

We want focused, meaningful, working prototypes that show how Bluevoir can combine AI innovation with enterprise-grade thinking.

Let’s build something bold.
