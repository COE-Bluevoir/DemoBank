# Bluevoir AI + Pega Hackathon 2026

## 1. Overview

Bluevoir is launching an internal hackathon to spark bold, practical, and showcase-ready innovation across the company.

This is not just an idea contest. Each team is expected to build and demonstrate a **working prototype**.

The hackathon is designed to encourage:

- AI-led innovation
- strong business relevance
- Pega-enabled or hybrid enterprise solutions
- reusable client showcase assets
- internal IP creation

## 2. Core Positioning

This hackathon is built around one simple challenge:

**Build the next meaningful AI-powered solution that Bluevoir can proudly showcase.**

### Strategic direction

- `BFSI` is the preferred industry focus
- `Non-BFSI` ideas are allowed
- AI is mandatory
- Pega is strongly encouraged
- Hybrid applications are strongly encouraged

### What “good” looks like

A strong submission should:

- solve a real business problem
- demonstrate clear AI usage
- show enterprise workflow thinking
- leverage Pega where it adds orchestration, workflow, case management, or decisioning value
- be compelling enough to showcase internally or externally

## 3. Participation

### Team structure

- Total teams: `10–11`
- Team size: `3–4 members`
- Participation: internal employee teams only
- Teams are already pre-divided

### Working model

This hackathon will run in **2 phases**:

1. `Kickoff and concept alignment`
2. `Build sprint and prototype demo`

## 5. Theme Direction

## Preferred focus: BFSI innovation

Teams are strongly encouraged to focus on:

- customer onboarding
- servicing
- lending
- claims
- fraud
- collections
- risk and compliance
- relationship manager productivity
- branch or operations productivity

## Open innovation allowed

Non-BFSI ideas are allowed if they clearly satisfy one or more of the following:

- strong strategic relevance to Bluevoir
- reusable Pega + AI pattern
- strong hybrid enterprise architecture
- reusable internal accelerator or showcase IP

## 6. Mandatory Rules

### Rule 1: AI is mandatory

Every submission must contain a meaningful AI component.

This can include:

- copilots
- assistants
- document intelligence
- summarization
- recommendation engines
- agentic workflows
- intelligent decision support
- knowledge retrieval
- conversational guidance

AI cannot be included only as a label. It must be a real and demonstrable part of the solution.

### Rule 2: Pega is strongly encouraged

Pega is not mandatory, but teams are strongly encouraged to use it wherever it adds value:

- workflow
- case management
- orchestration
- approvals
- exception handling
- enterprise decisioning
- human-in-the-loop control

### Rule 3: Hybrid apps are strongly encouraged

Teams are encouraged to combine:

- frontend apps
- APIs
- copilots
- Pega workflows
- external AI services
- enterprise integration patterns

### Rule 4: Working prototype required

Each team must submit a working prototype by final demo day.

The prototype does not need to be production-grade, but it must be demonstrable and coherent.

### Rule 5: Business relevance matters

Teams must clearly explain:

- who the user is
- what problem is being solved
- why the solution matters
- why this is relevant to Bluevoir

### Rule 6: No unsafe or unauthorized usage

Teams must not:

- use confidential production data
- expose credentials
- directly use restricted client environments without approval
- present a solution with unclear data/security posture

### Rule 7: Clarity beats breadth

Teams should prefer:

- one sharp, well-executed use case

over:

- many shallow features with weak demoability

## 7. Suggested Tracks

### Track 1: BFSI Innovation

For teams solving banking, financial services, insurance, compliance, lending, servicing, claims, fraud, or operations problems.

### Track 2: Pega + AI Hybrid Solutions

For teams combining Pega with AI, modern UX, orchestration, APIs, automation, or enterprise assistants.

### Track 3: Open Enterprise Innovation

For bold ideas outside BFSI that still create strong showcase value and strategic relevance for Bluevoir.

## 9. Judging Framework

Judges and prize details will be finalized separately.

Recommended judging criteria:

- `Innovation and originality` – 20
- `Business relevance / strategic fit to Bluevoir` – 20
- `AI depth and usefulness` – 20
- `Pega or hybrid architecture leverage` – 15
- `Prototype quality and demo readiness` – 15
- `Scalability / reusability / showcase potential` – 10

Total:

- `100 points`

Prize details are currently to be finalized.

- Top `3` teams will win prizes

- Best Pega + AI Hybrid Solution
- Most Innovative BFSI Use Case
- Best Showcase Potential

## 11. Organizer Guidance

### What to encourage in the kickoff session

- pick a real problem
- define one strong demo scenario
- keep scope tight
- use AI meaningfully
- use Pega where it adds enterprise value
- think about how the solution can be showcased after the event

### What to discourage

- vague “AI for everything” ideas
- oversized solutions with no working prototype
- solutions that are visually impressive but business-light
- solutions that use Pega only symbolically with no real role

## 12. Recommended Messaging

Use this line across communications:

> Build a bold, working AI-powered solution that Bluevoir can showcase with pride — with BFSI as the preferred focus, Pega as a strong enabler, and hybrid innovation encouraged.
