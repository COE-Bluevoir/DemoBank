# Hackathon Rules and Themes

## Official Theme Statement

Bluevoir teams are invited to build bold, working prototypes that combine AI-driven innovation with real business relevance.

### Strategic guidance

- BFSI is the preferred focus
- non-BFSI ideas are allowed
- AI is mandatory
- Pega is strongly encouraged
- hybrid apps are strongly encouraged

## Theme Buckets

### 1. BFSI Innovation

Suggested idea areas:

- account onboarding
- customer servicing
- loan origination
- fraud and risk
- KYC / AML / compliance
- claims and disputes
- collections and exception handling
- advisor / RM / operations productivity

### 2. Pega + AI Hybrid Solutions

Suggested idea areas:

- case-led copilots
- agent-assisted operations
- workflow + AI orchestration
- human-in-the-loop review systems
- intelligent employee workbenches
- modern UX on top of enterprise workflow

### 3. Open Enterprise Innovation

Suggested idea areas:

- internal productivity
- enterprise knowledge assistants
- cross-functional workflow automation
- agentic review / approvals
- document-heavy operations
- platform accelerators reusable by Bluevoir

## Rules

### Mandatory

- Teams must work within their assigned team structure.
- Every submission must include meaningful AI capability.
- Every team must deliver a working prototype.
- Every team must explain business value clearly.

### Strongly encouraged

- Use Pega meaningfully where it adds workflow or orchestration value.
- Build hybrid applications where appropriate.
- Think about client showcase potential and future reuse.

### Allowed

- BFSI and non-BFSI ideas
- web apps
- copilots
- mobile/web hybrid prototypes
- workflow accelerators
- internal enterprise tools

### Not allowed

- use of confidential production client data
- unsafe handling of secrets or credentials
- fake AI claims with no real implementation
- pure PowerPoint ideas without a working prototype

## What “meaningful AI” means

Good examples:

- document extraction and reasoning
- assistant-guided user flows
- recommendation and decision support
- summarization and exception review
- knowledge retrieval
- workflow-aware agents

Weak examples:

- adding a chatbot with no clear business purpose
- using AI only for cosmetic text generation
- including AI branding without user value

## What “meaningful Pega usage” means

Good examples:

- workflow and case orchestration
- review routing
- approvals
- exception handling
- decisioning
- enterprise audit/governance support

Weak examples:

- mentioning Pega in slides only
- using Pega with no important role in the solution

## Suggested Team Strategy

To maximize impact:

- choose one clear user problem
- define one hero demo scenario
- keep the flow simple enough to finish
- show where AI creates value
- show where Pega or hybrid architecture creates enterprise strength

## Submission expectation

Each team should answer:

- What problem are we solving?
- Who is the user?
- Why does this matter?
- Where is AI used?
- Where is Pega used?
- Why is this relevant to Bluevoir?
- What does the prototype actually do today?
