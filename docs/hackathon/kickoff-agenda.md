# Kickoff Agenda

## Bluevoir AI + Pega Hackathon 2026 Kickoff

**Date:** Saturday, August 8, 2026

## Suggested 90-minute agenda

### 1. Welcome and context – 10 min

- why we are running this hackathon
- what kind of innovation we want to encourage
- how this can help Bluevoir create reusable showcase assets

### 2. Hackathon vision – 10 min

- AI-first innovation
- BFSI preferred, not mandatory
- Pega strongly encouraged
- hybrid apps strongly encouraged
- working prototype expectation

### 3. Themes and examples – 15 min

- BFSI innovation
- Pega + AI hybrid solutions
- open enterprise innovation

### 4. Rules and judging – 15 min

- mandatory AI
- prototype requirement
- business relevance
- allowed/non-allowed boundaries
- judging dimensions

### 5. Team breakout instructions – 5 min

Each team should leave the session with:

- one problem statement
- one proposed solution
- one architecture direction
- one clear demo scope

### 6. Team breakout / concept alignment – 25 min

Suggested team discussion points:

- who is the user?
- what problem are we solving?
- why now?
- how does AI add value?
- where does Pega help?
- what can we realistically demo by next weekend?

### 7. Concept submission instructions – 10 min

Teams submit:

- team name
- title
- problem statement
- solution summary
- AI usage
- Pega usage
- business relevance

## Organizer close

Use this message:

> Keep it sharp. Keep it useful. Keep it demoable. One strong idea with a working prototype will beat five weak ideas every time.
